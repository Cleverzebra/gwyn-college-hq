Why the Bowdoin interview is this week the short version

Checked on Bowdoin's own booking page on Aug 1: open interview slots on Wed 8/5 and Thu 8/6, then nothing after Aug 21. The September to November schedule is not posted yet, and alumni interviews do not start until Oct 1, which is after preseason begins. So this week is the clean shot.

Bowdoin's own wording: whichever way you interview, on campus or online, with a student, staff member, or alum, "it all counts as an official interview and becomes part of your application." The on-campus one also comes with a lunch in Brunswick and a second look at the campus with senior-year eyes.

If this week falls apart: virtual Zoom slots run Aug 10 to 14, which works from Chautauqua.

Common App, essay, and supplements links + how supplements work

Open the Common App  The 7 essay prompts ↗

It opened Aug 1, and your account rolls over to this year when you log in. The main essay is 650 words max. Prompts 1, 5, and 7 fit the bridge-building and community thread best.

Where supplements live: add a college inside the Common App (College Search tab, then add), and that school's extra questions and supplemental essays show up under its own Writing section. That list is the real one, better than anything else online. Add Bowdoin first, since its supplement is the one that matters most.

The essay is broken into seven small steps across the month in the calendar above, roughly 20 to 30 minutes each. No single day asks you to write the whole thing.
