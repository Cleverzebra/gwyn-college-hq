Apply + SAT

Common App, essay, supplements, and the SAT where things stand

Open the Common App  The 7 essay prompts ↗

The essay is started, which is the hard part. The remaining path is short: finish a full draft, hand it to one adult reader, mark three fixes from their comments, fix them, paste it into the Common App. One 25-minute sitting at a time; the calendar tells you which day.

Supplements: add a college inside the Common App and its extra essays appear under that school's Writing section, which is the authoritative list. Add Bowdoin first. Write supplements only for schools that survive Sunday.

SAT: you already hold a 1520, so the retake is a choice, not a must. Facts for Sunday's decision: Brown now requires scores (your 1520 submits fine, it sits in their middle range). Every other school on your list is test-optional, and the ones that take scores use your best sections across dates, so a retake cannot lower what they see. If Sunday's answer is yes: the Oct 3 test is the one that fits ED. Register by Fri Sept 18 at collegeboard.org; scores return Oct 16, a month before Bowdoin's deadline. The Nov 7 test reports too late for ED, fine for regular decision.

Practice that fits your week: the SAT Coach on the home computer (also in the Dock as SAT Coach). One 15-minute adaptive set on days without a drive. It picks the next step for you, so there is nothing to plan.
